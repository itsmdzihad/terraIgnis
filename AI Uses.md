# How We Used AI

AI was a helping hand throughout the TerraIgnis project. It supported our
research, creative work, and development, while the team guided the work,
reviewed the results, and made the decisions.

## Research — Gemini Deep Research

Gemini Deep Research helped the researcher explore the fire-monitoring
problem, find useful background material, organize findings, and identify
questions worth investigating further. It served as a research assistant;
important facts and claims were checked against relevant source material by
the team.

## Video scripts and storytelling — ChatGPT

ChatGPT helped brainstorm and refine narration, story structure, and video
scripts. The team shaped the final story so it reflected TerraIgnis, the
challenge, and the message we wanted to share.

## Voice generation — ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs text-to-speech was used to turn the approved narration into a voice
track. We chose a dedicated text-to-speech tool to help produce clear,
expressive narration for the project video.

## Cover image, banner, and visual assets — Gemini

Gemini's image-generation feature was used at times to create or refine visual
materials, including cover imagery, banners, and other visual assets. The team
selected and reviewed the results to keep them in line with TerraIgnis's
identity and presentation.

## Planning and coding — Codex ,ChatGPT and Antigravity

Codex and ChatGPT worked alongside the developers as planning and coding
assistants. They helped us plan tasks, think through architecture and
implementation choices, understand errors, and draft or refine code. The team
directed the development, reviewed changes, integrated them into the
application, and remained responsible for the final technical decisions.
